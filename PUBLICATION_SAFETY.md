# Publication Safety Gate
**Never commit or publish:** patient identifiers; unpublished patient-level data; DSMB closed-session materials; unpublished trial results; restricted protocol details; internal emails; institutional documents; collaborator correspondence; secrets/keys/tokens.

Before publishing every essay confirm:
1. Patient data: no identifiers or re-identification clues.
2. Study/trial: no confidential findings, protocol details, or unreviewed results.
3. Collaborators: no private conversations or implicit quotations.
4. Figures/numbers: publicly citable sources or clearly labeled synthetic values.
5. Inference: facts and author interpretation distinguished.
6. Permissions: images, tables, and quotations cleared.
7. Disclaimer: personal views, not institutional positions.

If uncertain, do not publish. Use a completely synthetic vignette or general problem. `draft: true` hides generated pages but **does not make GitHub repository contents secret**.
